OPIS (≤4000):
Planning a night out, but sorting out the time, the place and who's coming across five chats is a headache? Squad is a group planner for your crew: create an invite (a "drop"), send the link and see right away who's in. Friends reply with one tap, no app install needed.

⚡ HOW IT WORKS
• Create a drop: what, where and when
• Send the link in any messenger or by text, or show a QR code
• Friends tap IN, LATE or OUT
• See who's coming, who's running late and by how many minutes, who's out

🎯 ANY KIND OF PLAN
Pizza, sports, house party, club, bar, coffee, movies, gaming, walks, trips, birthdays and other occasions. A hangout can repeat weekly, every two weeks or monthly.

💬 GROUP CHAT FOR YOUR CREW
Every drop has its own chat. "Where are we going?" polls, reactions, read receipts and typing indicators. No private messages.

👥 REGULAR GROUPS AND CONTACTS
Create a group with a chat that never disappears: your class, team or neighborhood crew. Invite friends to your contacts and add them to drops and groups in one tap, no links or QR codes needed.

📍 PUBLIC DROPS NEARBY
Join open meetups within 20 km or create your own with limited spots. 16+ only.

🔔 REMINDERS AND CALENDAR
Get notified when someone replies or writes, and the whole crew gets a reminder 30 minutes before the start. Add a drop to your calendar in one tap.

📸 ALBUM AND COLLAGE
When the drop starts, a shared album opens. Photos from the hangout in one place and a ready collage for your story.

🔒 SAFETY
Report and block with a long press on a message, a profanity filter, and links that disappear 24 hours after the drop starts. For ages 13+ (under 16 with a parent's or guardian's consent).

🌍 Available in English, Polish, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Czech and Ukrainian. Free to use.
