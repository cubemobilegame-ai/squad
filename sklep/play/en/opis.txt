TITLE:
Squad: Hangouts with Friends

SHORT DESCRIPTION:
Plan hangouts with friends: one link, group chat, reply IN, LATE or OUT.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Squad is an app for organizing hangouts with your friends. Create an invite (a "drop") with a place and time, send the link and see right away who's coming. No more sorting out details across several chats.

How it works
• Create a drop: what you're doing, where and when.
• Send the link in a messenger or by text, or show a QR code.
• Friends reply with one tap: IN, LATE or OUT. They don't need to install the app to answer.
• You see the guest list live, including who will be late and by how many minutes.

A planner for every occasion
House party, night out, club, pizza after practice, a match, the movies, gaming, a walk, a trip, a birthday, an 18th or an anniversary. Make a hangout repeat weekly, every two weeks or monthly, or turn it into a surprise where the place is revealed an hour before the start.

Group chat for your crew
Every drop has its own group chat. Run a "where should we go?" poll, add reactions and see who has read a message. Chats are group-only, with no private messages.

Permanent groups and contacts
Create a group with a chat that doesn't disappear: a class, a team, a group of friends. Add people to your contacts, invite them with a single tap and send drops straight to your groups.

Public drops nearby
Browse open hangouts within 20 km as a list or on a map. Join one or create your own with a limited number of spots. Public drops are available for people aged 16 and over.

Graphics to share
Send your invite as a ready-made graphic: for Instagram Stories, WhatsApp, Messenger or a text. Pick from several styles and save the image to your gallery.

3D avatars and badges
Choose your own 3D avatar and earn badges for being active: hosted hangouts, showing up, being on time and more.

Reminders, calendar and widgets
Get a notification when someone replies or writes, and a reminder for the whole crew 30 minutes before the start. Add a hangout to your calendar in one tap. On your home screen, add a widget in three sizes with your next drop and a countdown, plus quick New drop and Chats buttons.

Album and collage
When a drop starts, a shared album opens. All the photos from the hangout are in one place and turn into a ready-made collage.

Safety and privacy
Report or block a user by holding a message. A profanity filter is on, and drop links expire 24 hours after the hangout starts. You can delete your account at any time in the app settings. The app is for people aged 13 and over (13 to 16 with a parent's or guardian's consent).

Squad is free and available in Polish, English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Czech and Ukrainian.
